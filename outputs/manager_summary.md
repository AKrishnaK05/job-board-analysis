# Student Job Board
## Recruitment Performance Summary

**Analysis Period:** July – September 2026

### Key Findings

**1. Acquisition Channel Performance**

Referral was the strongest overall acquisition source, generating 10.74 hires per 100 applications. However, the leading source varied across job categories. Search performed best in Data and Finance, referral in Design and Marketing, college in Operations, and email in Software. This indicates that acquisition strategies may benefit from being tailored to individual role categories.

**2. Application Completion & Shortlisting**

Applicants completing all four application steps had a shortlist rate of 48.39%, compared with 9.28% among applicants completing fewer steps. The difference was 39.10 percentage points, with the completed-application group showing approximately 5.21 times the observed shortlist rate.

**3. Employer Responsiveness**

Paid opportunities had a median first-reply time of 3 days, compared with 7 days for unpaid opportunities. No reply was recorded for 13.08% of paid applications and 29.61% of unpaid applications.

### Recommendations

- Evaluate acquisition channels separately for each job category to improve hiring efficiency.
- Explore application progress indicators and reminders to encourage completion of all four steps.
- Introduce automated employer reminders for unanswered unpaid-role applications after four days.

Monitor response times and no-reply-recorded rates over the following month to assess the effectiveness of the intervention.

### Considerations

Missing reply times indicate that no response was recorded at the dataset snapshot and do not necessarily represent permanent non-response. The observed relationship between application completion and shortlisting does not establish causation.
