# Student Job Board: Recruitment Performance Summary

**Analysis Period:** July – September 2026

## Key Findings

**1. Acquisition Channels**

Referral generated the highest overall hiring yield, with **10.74 hires per 100 applications**. However, performance varied by role category: Search led Data and Finance, Referral led Design and Marketing, College led Operations, and Email led Software. This suggests acquisition performance should be assessed by role category as well as overall.

**2. Application Completion**

Applicants completing all four application steps had a **48.39% shortlist rate**, compared with **9.28%** among applicants completing fewer steps. The difference was **39.10 percentage points**, and the observed shortlist rate was **5.21 times as high**. This is an association and does not establish that completion causes shortlisting.

**3. Employer Responsiveness**

Paid roles had a median first-reply time of **3 days**, compared with **7 days** for unpaid roles. No reply was recorded for **13.08%** of paid applications and **29.61%** of unpaid applications.

## Recommended Actions

- Assess acquisition channels by role category when planning recruitment campaigns.
- Explore progress indicators and reminders to encourage applicants to complete all four steps.
- Pilot automated reminders to employers when unpaid-role applications have no recorded reply after four days. Monitor response times and no-reply-recorded rates before expanding the approach.

## Data Considerations

The cleaned dataset contains **1,200 unique applications**. Six duplicate application IDs were exact duplicate records and were reduced to one record each. Fifteen applications have an unknown acquisition source. Finance recorded **1 hire from 103 applications**, with **14 applicants reaching interview**; this result merits monitoring but does not, by itself, demonstrate a tracking issue. Missing reply times indicate that no reply was recorded at the dataset snapshot, not necessarily permanent non-response.
