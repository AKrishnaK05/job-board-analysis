# Student Job Board: Recruitment Performance Briefing

**Analysis Period:** July – September 2026

### Key Findings
1. **Acquisition Channels:** Referral is the strongest overall source, generating **10.74 hires per 100 applications**. However, performance varies by category: Search leads Data (7.4%) and Finance (2.9%), Referral leads Design (23.1%) and Marketing (22.2%), College leads Operations (20.0%), and Email leads Software (11.4%).
2. **Application Completion:** Applicants finishing all four steps achieved a **48.39% shortlist rate** versus **9.28%** for those completing fewer steps—an absolute gain of **+39.10 percentage points (5.21x higher)**.
3. **Employer Responsiveness:** Paid roles had a median response time of **3 days** (13.08% unrecorded), whereas unpaid roles took **7 days** (29.61% unrecorded). **This gap is significant enough to act on**, as a 30% ghosting rate severely erodes student trust.

### Recommendation for Next Month
Implement an **employer response SLA with automated reminders after 4 days** for unanswered unpaid roles, paired with an **in-app application progress indicator** to encourage 4-step completion.

### Data I Would Not Trust
- **Pipeline Ingestion Flaws:** 6 duplicate application records and 15 untracked sources point to data ingestion leaks.
- **Finance Tracking Anomaly:** 1 hire across 101 applications despite 13 interviews suggests broken conversion tracking rather than true applicant quality.
- **Snapshot Censoring:** Late-September applications with blank response times reflect in-flight status rather than permanent non-response.
